Description

RunRainRun! is a first-person 3D low-poly action-adventure where you must survive a violent storm, defeat 4 unique enemies, steal their abilities, and face off against a giant rain golem in a final battle under acid rain.

Every enemy you defeat gives you a “cheap copy” of their power—an unstable but useful version you can turn to your advantage. As the storm worsens and the rain turns deadly, you’ll need to master movement, powers, and umbrella mechanics to stay alive.

My main role in this project was as the Unity developer, responsible for implementing core mechanics, enemy behaviors, environmental interactions, and polishing the overall gameplay experience throughout development.
